Violin for Beginners

Your first step into the beautiful world of violin playing

About This Course

Designed specifically for those picking up a violin for the first time, this beginner course takes you from absolute zero to playing beautiful melodies with confidence.

What You'll Learn

  • Proper Posture — how to hold the violin and bow correctly
  • Basic Bowing Techniques — detaché, legato, and string crossing
  • Finger Placement — accurate intonation from the start
  • Reading Music — understand sheet music and rhythm
  • Your First Pieces — play simple songs by the end of the first month

Course Structure

The course is structured in progressive phases, each building on the previous one:

  • Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4): Instrument care, posture, open strings
  • Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8): Left hand finger placement, simple scales
  • Phase 3 (Weeks 9-12): Bowing techniques, first complete pieces
  • Phase 4 (Weeks 13-16): Dynamics, expression, and performance prep

Prerequisites

None! Just bring your enthusiasm. We'll help you choose or rent the right instrument.
