Jela Spitkova Violin Master Class — Hangzhou

We were honored to host the internationally acclaimed violinist Jela Spitkova for an exclusive master class in Hangzhou. Happy Children's Day! — Music Makes the World Different

Celebrating Children's Day with a special message: music makes the world different. At Elephant Music, we believe every child deserves the gift of music.
