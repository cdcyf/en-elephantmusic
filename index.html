Our Story

Originated from Taipei

Founded in Taipei in 2000, Elephant Music has dedicated over two decades to music education, teaching system research, and comprehensive music industry investment. We have accompanied countless students on their musical journeys.

Rent a Violin, Save the Earth

Since 2006, we've expanded violin rental to lower learning barriers. From beginner to professional instruments, we serve every customer while contributing to resource conservation.

Taiwan & Hangzhou Collaboration

In 2016, we expanded to Hangzhou to promote our teaching system and train instructors. The S&L brand represents our cross-strait collaboration ‚Ä?combining Taiwan's quality with Hangzhou's service.

Education System & Publications

Based on psychological age characteristics, we have exclusively developed the Elephant Music Teaching System, designing customized courses for all age groups and needs. Our "Breathing Music Class Series" for office workers and seniors focuses on stress relief and musical appreciation.

Teacher Zhang Jiazhen has published nearly 20 violin books including Easy Violin Tutorial and Feihua Etudes. Teachers Chen Yuzhi, Li Jiahua, and Zhuang Shuting have co-published nearly 10 early childhood music education books including Music Story House and Colorful Notes. The Easy Violin Tutorial is recognized by professionals worldwide.
