About Elephant Music

Two decades of musical excellence — from Taipei to the world

Our Journey

2000 — Founded in Taipei

Elephant Music Ltd. was established in Taipei, dedicated to music education, teaching system research, and comprehensive music industry investment. We specialized in violin training, rental, and sales from day one.

2006 — Rental for the Planet

We launched our violin rental program, making instruments accessible to everyone while promoting environmental sustainability through instrument sharing.

2016 — Cross-Strait Expansion

We expanded to Hangzhou, bringing our teaching system to mainland China and launching the S&L brand — a symbol of collaboration between Taiwan and Hangzhou.

2021 — Our Own Factory

Construction began on our modern instrument factory in Tongxiang Economic Development Zone, Zhejiang Province, marking a new chapter in our manufacturing capabilities.

Our Brand Philosophy

At Elephant Music, we believe that music is not just an art — it's a lifelong companion. Our mission is to make quality instruments and professional music education accessible to everyone.

With the Elegant brand as our foundation, we launched S&L — meaning your most "Special" gift and most "Lasting" companion. Combining Taiwan's quality craftsmanship with Hangzhou's dedicated service, we strive to be the most trusted musical instrument brand in the Chinese-speaking world and beyond.

"Every instrument tells a story. Let us help you write yours."

Our Instruments

S&L Brand Violins

Our own brand of handcrafted violins, designed and quality-controlled by our team, offering exceptional value from student to professional levels.

German GÖTZ — Exclusive Distributor

We are the exclusive distributor of GÖTZ violins in China. Since 1923, GÖTZ has been synonymous with German precision and musical excellence.

Italian Master Luthier Violins

Our exclusive collaboration with Italian master luthier Adriano brings the tradition of Italian violin-making to our customers.

Petrof & S&L Pianos

We offer Petrof Fibich grand and upright pianos from the Czech Republic, alongside our own S&L piano line.

Our Teaching Philosophy

Our faculty members began their musical journeys in childhood, studied under renowned masters, and graduated from prestigious international music conservatories. They have won awards in domestic and international competitions and bring rich teaching experience, performance skills, and multilingual capabilities to every lesson.

Our students have won international competitions and been admitted to renowned music conservatories worldwide. But more importantly, they've discovered a lifelong love for music.
